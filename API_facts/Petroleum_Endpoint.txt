Petroleum Endpoint:

Basics: 
    Petroleum Refiner basically converts(Refines) "Crude Oil" (Oil found in ancient organism or Fossil, hence also called Fossil fuel)
    into Petroleum Products e.g 

    Petroleum Products:
        1. butane(lighters, stoves) <85℉
        2. gasoline blending components (finished motor fuel) 85-185 ℉
        3. naphtha(used in making Plastics, gasoline) 185-350 ℉
        4. Kerosene, jet fuel (also used in lanterns, portable heaters) 350-450 ℉
        5. distillate (diesel, heating oil, kerosene) 450-650 ℉
        6. heavy gas oil(Mostly used in Marine vessels) also breaks down gasoline and diesel 650-1050 ℉
        7. Residual oil (Large ships, industrial power plants for electricty generation, space heating(large commercial boilers)) 1050 > ℉

    Conversion:
        most widely used method is "Cracking" it uese heat, pressure, catalysts, and sometimes hydrogen to "Crack" heavy hydrocarbon molecules into
        into lighter ones. 

        Cracking Unit: one or more tall, thick walled, rocket_shaped reactors and a network of furnaces, heat exchangers, and other vessels. 

        Complex refineries may have one or more types of crackers, including fluid catalytic cracking units and hydrocracking/hydrocracker units.

    Alkylation:
        Makes gasoline components by combining some of the gaseous byproducts of cracking. Basically reverse cracking 
    
    Reforming: 
        Uses heat, moderate pressure, and catalysts to turn naphtha
